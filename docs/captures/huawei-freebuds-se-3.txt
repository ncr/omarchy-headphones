Owner: defkode
Model: HUAWEI FreeBuds SE 3 (BTFT0016L), firmware 1.0.1.210
Date: 2026-11-13
Source: tools/huawei_probe.py on RFCOMM channel 1. Read only: the probe
  sends no set. Three sessions: the first with nothing moved, the second
  and third guided step by step by a desktop notification every 12 s or
  14 s.
Redactions: the serial numbers in the device-info answer (tags 09 and 18)
  are masked "**" by the probe; the two address values this model adds to
  that answer (tags 05 and 1b) are masked the same way by hand. The 0a 0d
  frames, a JSON event log this model sends in bursts as a bud is moved,
  are counted and left out: the bridge does not read them and their fields
  were not examined for identifiers. Python's traceback on a reset channel
  is replaced by one line saying so. Every other byte is verbatim.
  Timestamps are the probe's own elapsed clock, kept for ordering, not a
  timing model.

The battery frames are the FreeBuds SE 2's, byte for byte in layout: the
empty 01 06 as the channel opens, the unasked 01 27, and the 01 08 answer
with tags 1 to 4. So is the lid: 2b 5f 01 and a bud at 0 as it closes,
2b 5f 00 and the level again as it opens. New on this model: the channel
was reset when the lid first closed on the right bud (sessions 2 and 3),
and not when it closed on it a second time (session 3).
