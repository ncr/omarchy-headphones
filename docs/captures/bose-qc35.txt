The headset was connected to this machine and nothing else held the channel:
the plugin was not installed while any of this was recorded.

Every line marked `<<< RAW` is a socket read logged at receipt, before any
framing; `<<< FRAME` is what the length field then took out of the buffer.
A frame that arrived split across two reads therefore appears as two RAW
lines and one FRAME line. Timestamps are seconds since the section started
and mark receipt, not response latency: each step waits a fixed collection
window before moving on, so a reply's timestamp is bounded by that window,
not measured against the request.

Redactions: the `Name` and `Alias` fields of the SDP listing are the owner's
own words and are removed. Nothing else is removed, and nothing is
reconstructed — a byte absent from the wire is absent here.

Omissions: channels 2 and 9, the QC45's fallback candidates, were never
reached on this headset, because channel 8 answered the [0.1] probe first.
They are untried here, not refused.

SECTION D — naming the two strengths
====================================

The wire says 0, 1 and 3. It does not say which of 1 and 3 cancels more, and
the published third-party tables for this protocol say the opposite of what
this headset does, so they are not used.

The owner ranked the three values by ear with the values unnamed during the
test: state 1 = 0x00, state 2 = 0x01, state 3 = 0x03, twelve seconds each,
two rounds, no music, with constant background noise. His answer was that
state 3 was the quietest and state 1 the loudest, giving

    0x00  off
    0x01  low     the weaker strength
    0x03  high    the stronger strength

The run follows. What it proves on its own is only that the three values were
driven and restored; the naming rests on the owner's report of hearing them.

Also in this section: a 40-second window with nothing sent, to see whether
the headset announces a change made on the headset itself. It recorded no
unsolicited [1.6] frames — but the owner confirmed he did not operate the
control during the window, so this is UNTESTED, not a negative result. The
bridge polls every four seconds, which covers it either way.
