Redmi Buds 5, Fast Pair Message Stream (df21fe2c-2515-4fdb-8886-f12c4d67927c), in
three sessions on one pair. Address masked. The earbuds' own app (Xiaomi Earbuds)
and the RCSP read in redmi-buds-5.txt showed the true levels each time.

1. 2026-10-14, gfps-reader, 25 s and then 150 s (the owner moved a bud out of the case
   and back during the second): the channel opens and the reader prints, once each,

   {"address":"AA:BB:CC:00:00:01","stream":true,"single":false,"battery":-1,...,"modelId":"2b677d"}
   {... "bleAddress":"00:00:00:00:00:00"}
   {... "firmware":"0.0.1.0"}

   and nothing after: no battery frame.

2. 2026-10-23 10:08, gfps-reader, with the left bud not working (no sound from it,
   and no level for it in the widget; it worked again after both buds went into the
   case with the lid shut for a few seconds). The first three lines are as above,
   then:

   {"address":"AA:BB:CC:00:00:01","stream":true,"single":true,"battery":126,
    "batteryCharging":true,"left":-1,...,"modelId":"2b677d","bleAddress":"00:00:00:00:00:00"}

   A single battery byte, 0xFE: level part 0x7E = 126, bit 7 set. The reader printed
   it as 126% and charging. The widget showed "126%" in its tooltip and the bridge's
   left, right and case never reached the panel. (The raw frame is not in this
   session's output; 03 03 00 01 fe is what the reader's line implies.)

3. 2026-10-23, later the same day, tools/gfps_probe.py, both buds working, the widget
   showing L 65 / R 65 / case 61 from the RCSP bridge. The probe's own output:

   profile registered for df21fe2c-2515-4fdb-8886-f12c4d67927c
   NewConnection from /org/bluez/hci0/dev_AA_BB_CC_00_00_01 fd=9 props={dbus.String('Version'): dbus.UInt16(258, variant_level=1)}
   <<< 030100032b677d
   frame group=0x03 (DEVICE_INFO) code=0x01 len=3 payload=2b677d
   <<< 0302000600000000000003030002949403090007302e302e312e30030b0018000006dc0000000000000000000000000000000000000000
   frame group=0x03 (DEVICE_INFO) code=0x02 len=6 payload=000000000000
   frame group=0x03 (DEVICE_INFO) code=0x03 len=2 payload=9494
     BATTERY: battery=20% charging
   frame group=0x03 (DEVICE_INFO) code=0x09 len=7 payload=302e302e312e30
     FIRMWARE: 0.0.1.0
   frame group=0x03 (DEVICE_INFO) code=0x0b len=24 payload=000006dc0000000000000000000000000000000000000000
   ConnectProfile returned
   >>> 08110000

   The battery frame is 03 03 00 02 94 94: two bytes, each 0x94 = level 20 with
   bit 7 set, "20% charging", while the levels were 65 and 65.
   The reader reads one byte or three, so it says nothing for a frame like this.
   The probe's line "BATTERY: battery=20% charging" in the output above is from the
   probe before it was changed to say that two bytes are not a layout it reads.

What this shows, and what it does not: on this pair the stream's battery was absent
in one session, one byte 0xFE in another, and two bytes saying 20% charging in a
third, with the true levels 65-70% each time. Its battery is not to be believed, and
that is what the RCSP bridge is for. Why it changes (the left bud's link to the
right one was down in session 2; the earbuds' firmware reports 0.0.1.0 and an
all-zero BLE address throughout) was not tested.
